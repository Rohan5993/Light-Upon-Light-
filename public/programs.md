# Programs

Light Upon Light runs six programs focused on advocacy, accessibility, education, and equality for differently-abled people.

## Enlighten

In-school educational program that helps primary and secondary students build empathy, inclusion, and awareness toward differently-abled individuals through interactive lessons and hands-on wheelchair familiarity.

- Schedule: In-school sessions
- Location: Greater Seattle Area

## Big Light, Little Light

Mentorship matching older differently-abled mentors (Big Lights) with younger mentees (Little Lights), plus monthly virtual and in-person community sessions.

- Schedule: 2nd & last Monday monthly
- Location: Virtual + In Person

## Light Desserts

Accessible baking and kitchen life-skills sessions for youth and adults in a fully accessible kitchen.

- Schedule: 1st & 3rd Thursday monthly
- Location: Accessible Kitchen

## Signs of Our Light

Lived-experience accessibility assessments of public spaces, businesses, schools, and community organizations, with actionable recommendations.

- Schedule: By appointment
- Location: Community Spaces

## Sending Light Abroad

Collects, refurbishes, and delivers mobility aids (wheelchairs, walkers, crutches, canes) to underserved communities worldwide through local nonprofit partners.

- Schedule: Ongoing
- Location: Global

## Meet Our Light

Personalized in-person donation pickup in the Greater Seattle Area, often including a differently-abled participant so giving includes human connection.

- Schedule: By appointment
- Location: Greater Seattle Area
