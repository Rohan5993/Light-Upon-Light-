# Donate

Your donation helps Light Upon Light advance advocacy, accessibility, education, and equality for differently-abled people.

## Impact

Gifts support in-school education, mentorship, accessible life-skills programs, accessibility assessments, mobility-aid distribution, and community connection through Meet Our Light.
