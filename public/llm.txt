# Light Upon Light

> Light Upon Light is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 99-2690459) based in Redmond, Washington, dedicated to advocacy, accessibility, education, and equality for differently-abled people. Founded by Ronahi Zebari. Official website: https://thelightuponlight.org/

Light Upon Light serves the Greater Seattle Area (Everett and Redmond, Washington). Contact: Info@thelightuponlight.org | 206-766-0884. Address: 16305 NE 87th St, Redmond, WA 98052.

We are not affiliated with similarly named conference or religious event brands that also use the phrase “Light Upon Light.”

## Programs

- [Enlighten](https://thelightuponlight.org/programs.md): In-school education for empathy and inclusion
- [Big Light, Little Light](https://thelightuponlight.org/programs.md): Mentorship between differently-abled mentors and mentees
- [Light Desserts](https://thelightuponlight.org/programs.md): Accessible baking and life skills
- [Signs of Our Light](https://thelightuponlight.org/programs.md): Lived-experience accessibility assessments
- [Sending Light Abroad](https://thelightuponlight.org/programs.md): Refurbished mobility aids for underserved communities
- [Meet Our Light](https://thelightuponlight.org/programs.md): Personalized in-person donation pickup
