# Light Upon Light

> Light Upon Light is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to advocacy, accessibility, education, and equality for differently-abled people.

## Mission

We help differently-abled people through advocacy, accessibility, equality, and education while changing society's perception so every person is valued, included, and given the same opportunities as everyone else.

## Programs

- Enlighten: in-school education for empathy and inclusion
- Big Light, Little Light: mentorship between differently-abled mentors and mentees
- Light Desserts: accessible baking and life skills
- Signs of Our Light: lived-experience accessibility assessments
- Sending Light Abroad: refurbished mobility aids for underserved communities
- Meet Our Light: personalized in-person donation pickup
