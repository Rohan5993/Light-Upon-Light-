# Volunteer

Volunteer with Light Upon Light to support advocacy, accessibility, education, and equality for differently-abled people in the Greater Seattle Area and beyond.

## Ways to help

- Support program sessions (education, mentorship, baking, accessibility assessments)
- Help with community events and outreach
- Assist with donation pickup logistics (Meet Our Light)
- Offer professional skills (design, communications, operations)
