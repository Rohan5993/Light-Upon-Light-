# About Light Upon Light

Light Upon Light is more than a nonprofit — it is a movement for change for differently-abled people through advocacy, accessibility, education, and equality.

## Story

Founded after our Founder & CEO was denied a cup of tea because of her disability, Light Upon Light exists so differently-abled people are seen, valued, and included. The organization serves communities in the Greater Seattle Area and partners globally through mobility-aid work.

## Who we serve

Differently-abled individuals and families, schools and educators, businesses seeking real accessibility, volunteers, donors, and community partners.
