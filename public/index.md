# Light Upon Light

Light Upon Light is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit creating a future where differently-abled people are valued, included, and given the same opportunities as everyone else.

**Tagline:** Their Light Is Already There. Help Us Let It Shine.

## Mission

We help differently-abled people through advocacy, accessibility, equality, and education while changing society's perception so every person is valued, included, and given the same opportunities as everyone else.

## Our pillars

1. **Advocacy** — Stand beside differently-abled individuals and families; challenge barriers and ableism.
2. **Accessibility** — Make schools, businesses, parks, and public spaces usable for everyone.
3. **Education** — Replace fear and misconceptions with understanding, starting in classrooms.
4. **Equality** — Ensure the same dignity, respect, and opportunity for every person.
